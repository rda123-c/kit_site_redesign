Meet the Founders

William Fisher and Rob Barrett are the co-founders of Kit NA, based in Portland, Maine. Will brings deep craft beer expertise to Kit, having co-founded Austin Street Brewery in 2013 — one of the pre-eminent craft breweries in Portland, ME — before turning his attention to reimagining what non-alcoholic beer could be.

Together, Will and Rob set out to prove that choosing non-alcoholic doesn't mean settling for less. Drawing on years of brewing experience and a shared belief that great beer should be for everyone, they built Kit from the ground up — with real craft credentials and an uncompromising commitment to flavor.
